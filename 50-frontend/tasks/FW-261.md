## 1. What to build

**Hours:** 12 h FE · **Priority:** Medium · **Sprint:** S3 · **Phase:** 13 · **Stream:** FE

**As** an operator or roll-shop hand,
**I want** roll sets in the Tooling Inventory tab alongside dies, edgers and straighteners,
**So that** every tool on the line is traceable from one place.

> ⛔ **This card owes an un-priced mockup**, the same residual `FW-253` and `FW-256` carry.
> `[CE §2]`'s FE rate assumes an approved visual spec exists and there is none for this grid —
> because there is no client screenshot of it either. See `G87`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] **Roll Sets** added as the **fourth** *Choose Tool* option (`D-42`), after Dies, Edging Rolls and Straighteners. ⚠ The five Slitter-inherited options are **replaced**, not extended — `FW-003`
- [ ] The grid renders the roll-set columns **in the client's order**, once `Q92` supplies it
- [ ] `Machine Name` shows `FL1` or `FL2` only. ⚠ **No FL3 row** — FL3 uses a combination of the two, and its absence from the 31 Aug grids was intentional
- [ ] Mill and capstan rows distinguishable without reading the mount column — ⚠ whether they are one option or two is `Q92`'s second question, so **build the one-option form and keep the split cheap**
- [ ] Status renders the four lifecycle values; **`In Grinding` is not a boolean** — the same narrowness `G77` flags on `Edger.IsActive`
- [ ] ⚠ **Minimum text size 14 px.** Read at arm's length, like every shopfloor surface
- [ ] Dancers, entry guides, payoffs and spools **do not appear** on this tab. They are not tooling (`D-42`) and each has its own register

**Rate-card basis (§2):** one grid on an existing tab with a new dropdown option — below `FW-253`'s 24 h, which carries a screen and two history tabs = **12 h**. ⚠ **Rate assumes a mockup that does not exist**
