## 1. What to build

**Hours:** 12 h FE · **Priority:** High · **Sprint:** S2 · **Phase:** 6 · **Stream:** FE

**As** an operator or supervisor at a stopped line,
**I want** to record why the line is down when no run is open,
**So that** the 25 `Downtime` codes are not typed into a pause dialog that demands a run.

> ⛔ **`pause_run.js` deliberately refuses this job, and says so in its own header:** *"THE FOURTH
> BUCKET IS NOT HERE. Downtime (25 DWN## codes) is LINE-down time and belongs to
> LineDowntimeEvent, whose RunId is nullable; this dialog pauses a RUN. **Do not add a Downtime tab
> to it.**"* That leaves the fourth bucket with **no surface anywhere in the mockups**.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] ⛔ **No mockup exists.** Author `50-frontend/mockups/line_downtime.js` plus its thin `dashboard_*.html` launcher, following [`pause_run.js`](../mockups/pause_run.js)'s reworked pattern — **quick tiles over a select carrying the full code list** — on [`fw-modal.js`](../mockups/fw-modal.js). ⚠ **Edit the `.js`, never the launcher**
- [ ] Reached from the **line status board**, not from a run — there may be no run, no footage and no active operator session
- [ ] The **25** `Downtime` codes (16 inherited + 9 new) from `GET /reasons/downtime` filtered to the `Downtime` bucket. ⚠ **There is no bucket selector here** — one bucket, unlike the pause dialog's three
- [ ] `DWN29 Other` demands notes before the action button enables — the client-facing half of `CK_LineDowntimeEvent_NotesOther`
- [ ] **Supervisor override captured where the code carries it.** `Downtime` is the only bucket with override at all; the three change-tooling codes carry a delay buffer of 1
- [ ] An **open** downtime event is visible from the board and closable from the same dialog. ⛔ **Never stack dialogs** — close this one before opening a pause or WIP-rejection dialog; two live focus traps leave the operator unable to reach either
- [ ] Shopfloor rules honoured: **14 px minimum**, no scrolling dialog (oversized dialogs scale to fit — `.gb-modal` carries no `max-height`), and the **action** button in the footer carries an icon while the **dismiss** button does not
- [ ] Descriptions render **verbatim** from the seed — they are operator-facing labels and *"operators say spool"* is the recorded position

**Rate-card basis (§2):** Modal / dialog 12 h. ⚠ **The mockup is not in that rate** (AC 1) — `[CE §2]` prices dialogs against an approved visual spec
