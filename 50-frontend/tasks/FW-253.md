## 1. What to build

**Hours:** 24 h FE · **Priority:** Medium · **Sprint:** S3 · **Phase:** 13 · **Stream:** FE

**As** Maintenance,
**I want** one screen holding the die register and each die's life story,
**So that** a die is registered, reset, retired and re-thresholded without a database tool.

> ⛔ **This screen was MVP-2 until 2 Sep 2026 and is now MVP-1 (`Q91`).** `FR-240`–`FR-255` are
> MVP-1 requirements and [`DieManagement.md`](../../10-requirements/screens/DieManagement.md) is an
> MVP-1 specification. `FW-N07`'s contested MoSCoW split is settled the way its own story text
> always read — the **table** is *Must*, the **screen** is *Should* — and both are in scope.
>
> ⚠ **The 66 h that left MVP-1 on 11 Aug 2026 is not this 24 h.** That figure bundled the screen
> with a lifecycle service and a table; the split is `FW-251` 8 DB · [`FW-252`](../../40-backend/tasks/FW-252.md) 16 BE · this card 24 FE.
> **`FW-258` re-costs the whole return** — do not reconcile it here.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] ⛔ **No mockup exists for this screen, and the 24 h rate assumes one does.** `[CE §2]` prices the screen rate against *"the approved visual spec — no design time included"*. Author `50-frontend/mockups/die_management.html` **first**, to the `flat-wire-shopfloor` tokens, on `fw-modal.js` for its dialogs, at the **14 px** minimum, with no scrolling dialog and no stacked dialogs
- [ ] Inventory grid over `ToolingInventoryDie` carrying the client's 31 Aug 2026 field set — **`S/N`** · **`P/N`** · **`Location`** · **`Machine Name`** · **`ID(")`** · **`Max Imput Dia.`** · **`Pitch`** · **`Max ID(")`** · **`Lubrication Type`** · **`In Use`** — plus `DieAlpha`, `LastGrindingFeet` and `TotalFeetAllowed`
- [ ] ⚠ **`DieAlpha` is on no client grid at all** (`OI-141`). The field half of that item was resolved by building the **union** of both sets; the register half — one die register or two — **stays open**, and this screen must not be built in a way that presumes the answer
- [ ] Lifecycle bands over `LifecycleStatus` — `Active` · `In Service` · `In Grinding` · `Retired` — plus the **derived Spare band** from `InUse = 0`. ⚠ A `BIT` cannot express *In Grinding*; that is exactly `G77`'s point about `Edger.IsActive`, and why this column is a `VARCHAR`
- [ ] **`FR-252`'s two history tabs** — Replacement log and Run history — both fed by `GET /die/{dieAlpha}/history` off the one discriminated `DieHistory` table
- [ ] Register · reset · retire · edit-threshold actions, each capturing the reason `FR-249` / `FR-250` require. `FR-250`'s five retire reasons and `FR-248`'s two reset dispositions are **different vocabularies in one column** — the screen must not offer them interchangeably
- [ ] An **overdue** die (`LastGrindingFeet` past `TotalFeetAllowed`) renders as an operating state, and a die with a **`NULL` threshold** renders without one — no invented limit, no false warning
- [ ] ⛔ **The threshold bands are not chosen here.** `OI-12` — this screen's 65/80 % and Die Change's 60/85 % now read one table. Owned by [`FW-199`](../../60-delivery/tasks/FW-199.md)
- [ ] ⚠ **Edger and straightener inventory are out of scope** — `G77`. The client's Tooling Inventory tab carries **four** tool types (`D-42`) and only **dies and roll sets** have a schema. ⚠ **Roll sets are also out of scope for this card** — `ToolingInventoryRollSet` exists but its columns are `[PROPOSED]` pending `Q92` (`G87`), so its grid is a separate story
