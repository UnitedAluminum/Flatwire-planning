Where this drawing differs from the source

  • TKUP-1 reads “1,800 lbs” on the source. That is the working spool target — sized so two finished coils can be cut at FL2 (FR-130a). The equipment ceiling is 3,500 lb. Both are shown.
  • TKUP-2 reads “1,000 lbs” on the source. Revised up to 1,100 lb on 4 May 2026 (OQ-77). Drawn as 1,100 lb.
  • The edgers are not on the source drawing at all. D-27 puts two of them inter-stand — between S1/S2 and between S2/S3, entry-side, with no exit-side edger. They are vertical profiling rollers, not knives: nothing is trimmed and there is no scrap stream at the edger. S3 is a skim pass with no edging. FL1 has no edger.
  • FM2’s dancers are marked twice, deliberately. The source shows one before FM2 and one after it. D-28 instead places two dancers in the edger positions, each with a regular and a tension mode. That is still open — Q32, gap G35 — so both readings are shown and neither is presented as settled.
  • Roll diameters come from Stand.RollDiameterIn, not from the drawing: FM1 12.000″; FM2 8.000″ / 6.000″ / 6.000″ (D-26). The 8″ roller is S1 — there is no separate 8" Roller component and no fourth FM2 stand.
  • The step between the lines is drawn as decoupled, not as a conveyor. A spool leaves TKUP-1, is optionally annealed off-line, and is re-planned before it is loaded on the TPO. The only continuous path between the lines is FL3.
