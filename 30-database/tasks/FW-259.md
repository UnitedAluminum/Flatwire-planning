**As a** developer building the Tooling Inventory tab,
**I want** the roll-set register to hold the columns the client actually asked for,
**So that** the roll shop is not entering data against fields we invented.

> **The table is already built. This story is the reconciliation, not the build.**
> `ToolingInventoryRollSet` landed on 3 Sep 2026 with `D-42`, and the schema verifier is green at
> **40 tables · 64 FKs · 86 index statements**. What it does *not* have is client authority for its
> column set: dies, edgers and straighteners each arrived as a **pictured grid**, and roll sets
> arrived as **one sentence**. Every column is marked `[PROPOSED]` at four sites — the DDL comment,
> the schema document, `[MSP §5]` and `[DBD §6.2]` — and this card is what removes those marks.
