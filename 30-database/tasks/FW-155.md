## 2. The two details

### 2.1 The name understates the key, deliberately

`IX_FlatWireRun_LineId` is keyed `(LineId, Status)`. A reader checking the *name* against the
card's *"keyed `(LineId, Status)` — not `LineId` alone"* will conclude the index is wrong.

**It is not.** The DDL is authoritative for types, nullability and constraints, and the key is
in the `CREATE` statement at `:40`. ⚠ **Renaming it would break three citing documents for no
functional gain.**

### 2.2 AC 2 is the one thing still owed, and it is not a DB task

*"`GET /lines/status` execution plan uses it"* cannot be checked while
`LineStatusService.GetLinesStatusAsync` throws. **An index that exists is not an index that is
used** — a query written with a leading `Status` predicate, or one that selects columns forcing
a key lookup, may ignore it entirely.

⚠ **So this story is *built* but not *verified*.** The verification belongs with
[`FW-154`](../../40-backend/tasks/FW-154.md) —
capture the plan when that service body lands.

⛔ **Do not close this story on the artifact alone.** That is the mistake that makes an index a
decoration.
