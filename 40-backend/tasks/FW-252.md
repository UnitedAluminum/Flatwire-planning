## 1. What to build

**Hours:** 16 h BE · **Priority:** High · **Sprint:** S2 · **Phase:** 6 · **Stream:** BE

**As** Maintenance,
**I want** each physical die's life tracked against that die,
**So that** fitting a fresh die resets a counter the system can tell apart from its neighbour's.

> ⭐ **This story only exists because of the die split (`Q91`, 2 Sep 2026).** For five months
> `OI-41` recorded the accepted consequence *"two dies of one diameter share a counter, and
> fitting a fresh die resets nothing"*. **`OI-41` is closed and that consequence is retired** —
> `ToolingInventoryDie` is the per-tool register and `DieHistory` is its append-only life log.
> **`FR-233` / `D4` revert to their per-tool form**, and `TC-274` is executable for the first time.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] `DieLifecycleService` — **register · install · reset · retire · edit-threshold**, each writing exactly one `DieHistory` row with the matching `EventType` (`Install|Reset|Retire|ThresholdEdit|RunFootage`, enforced by `CK_DieHistory_EventType`)
- [ ] **`POST /diechange` validates the incoming die against `ToolingInventoryDie`** — an unregistered `DieAlpha` (`D-{size×1000}-{seq}`) is rejected, prompting Maintenance to register it — and writes `OldDieId` / `NewDieId`. **`TC-274` passes**
- [ ] ⚠ **The rejection reason changed.** MVP-1 previously rejected an unrecognised *size*; it now rejects an **unregistered tool**. A die of a known size that is not in the register is refused
- [ ] `FR-255` per-run footage accrues as a `RunFootage` row, and `ToolingInventoryDie.LastGrindingFeet` is maintained alongside it. ⛔ **`LastGrindingFeet` is deliberately denormalised** against `SUM(DieHistory.FootageAddedFt)` — this service owns keeping the two equal, and a reconciliation check belongs here, not in a report
- [ ] `CK_DieHistory_RunFootageHasRun` and `CK_DieHistory_FootageOnlyOnRunFootage` are exercised — a footage row without a run is refused, and no other event type may carry footage, so the Run-history tab cannot double-count a reset
- [ ] A **reset** zeroes `LastGrindingFeet` and stamps `LastResetBy` / `LastResetAt` (`FR-245`, `FR-248`), and records `RemovedFromLineDate`, `ReturnedReadyDate`, `InspectionDate` and `DieRoomSource` on the history row
- [ ] `GET /die/{dieAlpha}/history` serves **both** views `FR-252` needs from the one discriminated table — the **Replacement log** (`Install|Reset|Retire|ThresholdEdit`) and the **Run history** (`RunFootage`)
- [ ] `FR-249`'s reason is required on a threshold edit; `FR-250`'s retire reason is captured. ⚠ **`DieHistory.ReasonCode` is deliberately not `CHECK`-constrained** — two vocabularies share the column and neither is client-confirmed
- [ ] ⚠ **No threshold is invented.** `TotalFeetAllowed` is nullable until the client supplies it, and there is deliberately **no `CHECK` that `LastGrindingFeet <= TotalFeetAllowed`** — *overdue* is a real operating state the screen displays, not a data error the database refuses
- [ ] ⛔ **The 60/85 % vs 65/80 % band conflict is NOT resolved here.** `OI-12` escalated to a live defect on 2 Sep 2026 because Die Change's bands and Die Management's bands now derive from **one** table. Owned by [`FW-199`](../../60-delivery/tasks/FW-199.md); this service reads whatever it settles on
