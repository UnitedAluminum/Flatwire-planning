## 2. The four details

### 2.1 ⛔ Views, because three consumers need one number

The banner states it once and it is the design: *"the API, the reports and the certificate all
need the same number, and **a view is the only form all three can read**."*

⚠ **A service method would serve the API and leave the reports and the certificate to
re-implement the arithmetic** — three implementations of one figure, diverging silently. That is
the same failure mode as the six PLC tag copies, applied to a number.

⛔ **So the API reads the view too.** A handler that recomputes fulfilment "for efficiency"
re-creates the divergence the view exists to prevent.

⚠ **Note the tension with `[SVC §3.2]`'s MediatR-through-repository pattern** — the query handler
reads a **view**, which is legitimate (`FW-141`'s `ContextRepository` already reads
`sp_GetGaugeTrace`), and `[REQ]`'s certificate obligation is what makes it necessary here.

### 2.2 ⛔ Footage share, not parent count

*"Apportion a multi-parent coil by **footage share**, not by counting parents — a two-parent coil
is rarely 50/50."*

⚠ **The naive implementation is not obviously wrong.** With two parents, dividing by two gives a
number that looks right, sums correctly across the coil, and is only detectably wrong when
someone compares it to the footage ranges.

⛔ **`CoilTraceability` holds footage ranges → rod alphas** — the apportionment data is right
there. **Use the range widths.**

⚠ **And `G42`'s warning applies**: the genealogy is **weight-primary** and `SpoolTraceability`'s
footage bounds are **nullable**. So *"apportion by footage share"* has to handle a segment whose
footage bounds are absent — ⛔ **and it must not silently fall back to an equal split**, which is
the very thing the criterion forbids.

### 2.3 Two yields, and conflating them is a customer-facing error

| Figure | Formula | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Yield on metal **run** | `produced / consumed` | How well the material that went through the line converted |
| Yield on metal **issued** | `produced / allocated` | How well the material *committed* to the order converted |

⛔ **They differ whenever allocated ≠ consumed** — which is the normal case, since an allocation
is a plan and consumption is what actually ran.

⚠ **`OI-60` already owns "metallic yield per route"**, and `Q19` was re-homed there — so **yield
naming has history**. ⛔ **Never publish a column called `Yield`.** The card says *"named apart"*
and that is a schema-level instruction.

### 2.4 `Q53` reaches the certificate

*"Is fulfilment consumed or produced pounds — **and which does the certificate state?**"*

⛔ **The second clause is what makes this `Critical` in effect if not in label.** A certificate is
a **customer-facing document** backing the welding-wire obligation (`NFR012`) that put Phase 9 in
MVP-1 at all.

⚠ **And `Q10` compounds it**: pounds come from the footage→weight factor, which is `NULL`. **So
even after `Q53` picks a basis, the number is unavailable** until `Q10` lands —
[`FW-228`](FW-228.md)'s `P-206` requires **unknown, never zero**, and
that must propagate through the views rather than being coalesced away.

⛔ **A view with `ISNULL(weight, 0)` would turn "unknown" into "produced nothing"** on a customer
document.

## 4. Decisions this plan makes

> `P-##` is continuous across the repository; `P-01`–`P-248` precede this story.

### `P-249` — unknown propagates through the views and is never coalesced to zero

§2.4, extending `P-206` and `P-149`. On a **customer-facing certificate**, "produced nothing" and
"we cannot yet compute this" are different statements, and `ISNULL(weight, 0)` silently converts
the second into the first.

⛔ **Four columns now share this rule** — `RunReading.InSpec`, `SpcMeasurement.InSpec`,
`FW-228`'s factor, and these views. **It is a repository-wide discipline.**

### `P-250` — apportionment handles absent footage bounds explicitly, and never falls back to an equal split

§2.2. The genealogy is weight-primary with **nullable** footage bounds (`G42`), so the
apportionment input can be missing — and the fallback everyone reaches for is exactly the
parent-count split the criterion forbids.

**Fallback:** apportion by **weight** where footage is absent, if the weights are known; otherwise
**return unknown** (`P-249`). ⛔ **Never an equal split.**

### `P-251` — the status vocabulary is shared with `FW-227`, not re-declared

§3 step 7. *"Pending operator confirmation"* corresponds to `FW-227`'s `ThresholdReached`. **Two
vocabularies for one lifecycle drift**, and this one is read by the reports and the certificate.
