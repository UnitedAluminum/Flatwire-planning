## 1. What to build

**Hours:** 22 h BE · **Priority:** High · **Sprint:** S2 · **Phase:** 6 · **Stream:** BE

**As** a line supervisor,
**I want** line-down time recorded against the line,
**So that** a power outage, a fire drill or a wait for the previous operation has somewhere to go.

> ⛔ **44 of the client's downtime codes had nowhere to be recorded, and the reason is structural.**
> `RunPauseEvent.RunId` is `VARCHAR(20) NOT NULL` with an FK to `FlatWireRun`, `FootageAtPause` is
> `NOT NULL`, and all four `CK_RunPauseEvent_Outcome` values presume a run. The `Downtime` bucket's
> 25 in-scope codes are line-down time — *Power Outage*, *Fire Drill*, *Scheduled Maintenance*,
> *Waiting for Spool From Previous Operation* — **which is exactly when no run is open.**
>
> **`D-35` resolved it with a new table rather than by relaxing `RunPauseEvent`.** `RunPauseEvent`
> keeps its shape and narrows to the three run buckets (**47** codes); `LineDowntimeEvent` takes
> the other **25**. `WipRejection.RunId` has been nullable for precisely this reason all along.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] `POST /line/{lineId}/downtime` opens a `LineDowntimeEvent` — **line-scoped**, with `RunId` set only if a run happens to be open, and `NULL` otherwise
- [ ] `POST /downtime/{id}/end` closes it, stamping `EndedAt` and `EndedBy`. ⚠ **`DowntimeSeconds` is a computed column** — never written, and `NULL` while the event is open
- [ ] **`IsNonprodTime`, `DelayBufferMin` and `SupervisorOverride` are snapshotted from `DowntimeReason`** at the moment downtime starts, so retuning editable reference data cannot re-price history. Same rule as the pause path
- [ ] Only `DWN##` codes are accepted — `CK_LineDowntimeEvent_Code`. The other three buckets are [`FW-170`](FW-170.md)'s pause path, and `CK_RunPauseEvent_Bucket` rejects `Downtime` there. **The two endpoints must not each accept the other's codes**
- [ ] `DWN29 Other` requires notes — `CK_LineDowntimeEvent_NotesOther`, mirroring `SET23`/`RUN12`/`HDL15` on the pause side. ⚠ **`DWN29`'s `Nonprod Time` cell arrived blank** while every sibling in its bucket says `Yes`; the seed's choice stands and is not re-decided here
- [ ] `SupervisorOverrideBy` is only ever set with `SupervisorOverride = 1` (`CK_LineDowntimeEvent_Override`). ⚠ **`Downtime` is the only bucket carrying supervisor override at all**, and only three codes carry `Delay Buffer 1` — *Change Straightener Rolls*, *Change Dies*, *Change Edger Rolls*
- [ ] `LineStatus` is broadcast with the downtime reason, as pause is, and cleared on end
- [ ] ⚠ **Decide and record who may open and close a downtime event, and whether an open event survives a shift change.** A line can be down with no run, no footage and nobody at a dashboard — none of the existing event services has that shape
- [ ] ⚠ **`Bundle` and `Spool` in the seeded descriptions are operator words, not schema words.** *Waiting for Spool From Previous Operation* means `SpoolProcessing`; **never `Spool`**, which since `Q60` is the reusable stencilled article with no `Alpha` at all. Seed text is verbatim; do not let it steer the mapping
