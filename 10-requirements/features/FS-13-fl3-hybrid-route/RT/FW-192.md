## 1. What to build

**Hours:** 8 h RT · **Priority:** Medium · **Sprint:** S3 · **Phase:** 10 · **Stream:** RT

**As an** FL3 operator,
**I want** the trace to run unbroken from rod to coil,
**So that** traceability is continuous across the whole hybrid run.

> ⭐ **Re-pointed 9 September 2026 (`G120`).** `A3` is retired and `FR-120` superseded: **FL2
> broadcasts live gauge and width at 4 s.** ✅ **AC 1 gets easier and is no longer in tension with a
> `Must` requirement** — there is no historical switch to avoid, because there is no historical
> mode. ⛔ **But AC 3 is retired and the work it named has been replaced by something harder:** a
> hybrid run's one continuous series is now spliced from **two live sources at two cadences** —
> `FL1.PLC.AGC.*` at ~10 Hz for the FM1 end and `FL2.PLC.AGC.*` at **4 s** for the finishing end,
> since `D-47` puts FM2 under the FL2 controller ([`[PLC §5.2.3]`](../../../../20-architecture/PLCTagSpecification.md)).
> ⚠ **`G99` is the prerequisite risk, and it does not close**: FL3's 33 tag paths are still
> `FL3.*`, deliberately un-prefixed, and the whole block has to be re-pointed at FL1/FL2 addresses —
> FL3 is not in `IngestLines` at all. ⚠ **Hours not re-derived at 8 h**; the cadence splice is the
> part to size against once `G99` has an answer.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Continuous `GaugeReading` / `WidthReading` end to end — **no FL2 historical switch mid-run** — ✅ **and as of 9 Sep 2026 there is no such switch to make anywhere** (`FR-120` superseded)
- [ ] Weld events mid-run keep the traceability chain continuous
- [ ] ➕ **The series is ONE series across two cadences.** The FM1 end arrives at ~10 Hz and the finishing end at 4 s, so ⛔ **the join must not be drawn as a step change in the material** and ⛔ **the 4 s stretch must not be interpolated into a smooth line** — consume `FW-081`'s footage-stamped buffer (`P-344`), do not re-time the samples
- [ ] ~~The Live/Profile toggle is **available** on FL3, because a hybrid run genuinely has both~~ ⛔ **Retired 9 Sep 2026.** An FL3 run is **live end to end** — it never had "both", and that incoherence (the same FM2 real-time on FL3 and blind on FL2) is what retired `A3` (`G120`)
