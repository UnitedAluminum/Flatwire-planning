## 2. ⭐ Half the wizard has no requirement behind it

[`[RCI §3.1]`](../../../screens/RocCheckin.md) is the gate authority. It enables **Acknowledge &
Begin Check-in** on **eight** pre-flight checks:

| # | Check | On failure |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rod serial valid and in the coil master (`CHK006`) | Blocked |
| 2 | Rod not already checked in elsewhere (`CHK009`) | Blocked |
| 3 | Measured diameter within the alloy's min/max (`CHK007`) | Blocked, field highlighted |
| 4 | **Gross and net weight entered** | Missing fields highlighted |
| 5 | Payoff position present | Missing field highlighted |
| 6 | Visual inspection items pass (`CHK010`) | ⛔ **Hard block** → WIP Rejection, no bypass |
| 7 | Pre-run SPC diameter recorded | Blocked, field highlighted |
| 8 | A pass schedule has been resolved | Blocked — *No active pass schedule for this order* |

⛔ **Not one of the eight is a machine inspection.** Steps 4 (Die Block), 5 (Rolling Mill) and 6
(Lube & Safety) appear in the mockup and in `[TB §7]`'s AC, and **nowhere else**:

| They have no… | Measured |
|---|---|
| gate membership | `[RCI §3.1]`'s eight checks omit them, so AC 2 and the FRS define **different gates** |
| persistence | **none of the 19 tables** in `FlatWire_DDL_04_Runs.sql` / `_05_*.sql` holds a machine inspection. `RodCheckin` covers step 1 (four inspection columns + notes) and step 3 (`SpcM1In`, `SpcM2In`, computed `SpcOvalityIn`) and nothing else |
| contract field | `POST /checkin/rod` has no field for any of it |
| FL3 form | the six steps are shaped to FL1's machine set; nothing defines FL3's — §3 |

Counted from the mockup: **15 distinct `data-g` OK/NG/NA groups and 5 measured values** — four
carrying a spec tag (DB1 and DB2 die-ring diameters, FM1 roll gap and roll width) plus a free
lube-temperature reading. All twenty gate the button and are then discarded.

⛔ **Raised as `G125`.** The requirement is owed before the code is, so it is BA-first, then
[`FW-157`](../BE/FW-157.md) (BE) and [`FW-159`](../DB/FW-159.md) (DB).

⚠ **And the lube-temperature limit is dead even inside the mockup.** It renders *Target: 68–80 °F*
next to a free numeric input, is **not** an OK/NG group, and **no validator reads it**. Build it as
a real check against injected limits or drop the target text — ⛔ do not ship the appearance of a
limit that gates nothing.

### 2.1 The other traps, each measured

- ⛔ **`PS-1100-FL1-003` is the seed's negative case** — `Draft`, refused with `422
  SCHEDULE_NOT_ACTIVE` — and it is quoted as *the recommendation* in **three** places: the mockup,
  `[API §4.6]`'s worked request, and `[RCI §3.4]`'s worked example. **The FL1 happy path is
  `PS-1100-FL1-001`** (`[CMP §5.3]`, `[UIC §1]`). A mock seeded from the mockup asserts the
  opposite of the contract.
- ⛔ **Clone Dashboard 12's page skeleton, not DB2's.** `[SCR §5.6]`: DB2's mockup **inlines its own
  app bar and omits `flat-wire-topbar.js`**. Its `.global-bar`, `#switch-overlay`, `#more-overlay`
  and `.load-overlay` are demo scaffolding (`[UIC §3.7]`), not screen content — the real shell is
  already rendered by `flat-wire.component`.
- ⛔ **Do not port `flat-wire-fit.js`.** `[UIC §4.1]` gives six measured reasons; the screen
  **budgets** its height and neither scales nor scrolls. What the mockup gets right is the budget,
  not the scale.
- ⛔ **Tolerances are data, never constants.** The mockup hard-codes `0.370` / `0.380` / `0.003`.
  [`[VAL §7.5a]`](../../../../50-frontend/ValidationRules.md) is explicit that client-side
  validation *has never been written* and that **nothing here should be inferred from the mockups'
  markup**. Rule `CI-9` makes it four attributes — gauge, width, diameter, ovality. `Q22` +
  **`G121`** mean the values are per **vendor × size band** and ungrained, so `CHK007` cannot fire.
  See `P-11`.
- ⚠ **`G14`'s inspection half is live and the two sides disagree.** `[RCI §3.1]` check 6 and
  `[RCI §4.3]` say **three** items; `[API §4.6]` resolves the contract as **three at staging, four
  at check-in** (the connector-tag check belongs to check-in) and the DDL builds four `NOT NULL`
  columns. **Build four, and let the count be data** — `FW-134 §2` says the same.
- ⚠ **Gross and net weight are enterable.** `[RCI §3.1]` check 4 requires them *entered* with
  missing fields highlighted, and `POST /checkin/rod` carries both. Measured in the mockup: gross is
  `<input class="input" … disabled>` and net is a `.input-readonly` **div** — neither can be typed
  into. Two real, validated fields are owed.
- ⚠ **A non-recommended schedule requires a free-text reason** — `[RCI §3.4]` `[CONFIRMED]`, rule
  `CI-5`. The mockup collects none: `confirmSchedule()` writes a canned string, and step 2's
  validator never returns `failed`, so the hold bar that *does* have a reason field never opens.
- ⛔ **`GET /rod/{alpha}` is WITHDRAWN, not merely unhosted — `P-54` closed 10 Sep 2026 as option 3,
  re-specify** — and [`FW-233`](../BE/FW-233.md) **closed with no build**, releasing its 6 h.
  ⚠ **`Step-4`'s `getRod` was built against it and is removed** — see §5.1a.
  ⭐ **What replaced it changed again the same day, and this is the current answer.** `P-54` sent the
  coil master to *"the receiving team's own surface"* — ⛔ **which is not an endpoint, a service, a
  screen or a named contact in any of the four checkouts** — while `FR-064` / `CHK006` **survived the
  closure**, as `[API §4.3]`'s own kept-shape note says. A *Must* with no host is not re-specified.
  So `P-351` hosts the **check-in gate** here: ⭐ **`GET /checkin/rod/{alpha}`**
  ([`[API §4.22]`](../../../../40-backend/APIs.md), [`FW-N35`](../BE/FW-N35.md)) — existence plus the
  coil master, read-only over a `FlatWireDB` synonym on `CommonDB..coils`, creating nothing
  (`FR-530`). ⛔ **It is not `§4.3` under a new number**: no staging projection, no allocation set,
  no `diameterIn`.
  ⛔ **Three fields this plan used to claim from `GET /payoff/status` are NOT on `[API §4.4]`** —
  `scheduledMachineName`, `remainingWeightEstimateLb` and a per-rod `orderId`; `activeOrderId` is
  documented `null` while a line is `Idle`, which is the state at check-in. They belong to `OI-33`.
  What §4.4 **does** carry, and what `Step-7` uses it for, is **bay occupancy** — `position`,
  `state`, `rodAlpha`, `isWelded` — plus the staged bay's own alloy/temper/weights for the
  pre-fill. ⚠ `FW-158` is Phase 4 and **not started**, so the payoff half is still not live.
- ⛔ **The friendly error renders are only exercisable on the mock path.** `shared`'s
  `ErrorHandlerService` pops a modal on `errorDescription` and the subscriber's `next` never fires,
  and `CLAUDE.md` forbids manual error handling. Real-path behaviour is
  [`FW-131`](../../FS-01-angular-application-shell/FE/FW-131.md)'s toast-versus-inline decision,
  which `[VAL §7.5a]` already records as owed.
- ⛔ **Tabular data is plain Bootstrap `<table>` markup.** `lib-static-grid` / `lib-common-grid`
  break `D-06`, and `CommonGridModule` is deliberately not imported by `FlatWireModule`.

## 3. One shared Check-In screen for all three stations — the station is data

⭐ **`D-60` (10 Sep 2026): one component serves FL1, FL2 and FL3.** **FL1 rod · FL2 spool ·
FL3 rod.** This replaces the arrangement every other document still describes — DB2 for the two rod
lines and DB5 for FL2 — and it **retires [`FW-064`](../../FS-11-spool-lifecycle-fl2/FE/FW-064.md)**
(*Dashboard 5 — FL2 Spool Check-in*, 16 h, Phase 8) into this story. `FW-064` keeps its id and
forwards here; ⛔ it is not deleted.

⚠ **This was a new decision, not the recovery of an old one.** Nothing in the repository had
proposed merging DB2 and DB5 — they are separate in `[SCR §7.1]`, `[CMP §5.1]`, `[CMP §5.2]` and
`[SCR]` Appendix B. Two things nevertheless pointed at it, and they are the reason it is low risk:

- ✅ **`FR-094` already asks for the sharing in its own words** — *"Dashboard 5 shall show the FL2
  pass-schedule component table … read-only, **with the same mandatory confirm bar as Dashboard 2**."*
- ✅ **`[RCI]` was already one specification over two screens.** Its §4 opens *"The same five steps,
  with a different material and a different tag set"* and `[RCI §4.3]` tabulates the seven differences. The
  **sequence** was always shared; only the screens were split.
- ✅ **`FS-11` argues for this shape on its own screen.** Its §8: *"`D-55` made Dashboard 3 one
  component, so FL2 additions are **profile data**. A per-line fork here would undo the decision and
  break `FS-13` too."*

⚠ **One design note is deliberately overturned.** DB5's mockup removed the wizard on 2 Aug 2026
because *"DB5 is READ-heavy, not entry-heavy … tabbing it would hide the FL1 profile and the
traceability list, which are the two things `FR-092`/`FR-093` exist to put in front of the
operator."* The layout is now shared, so that note is superseded by `D-60` — and the concern behind
it is answered rather than ignored: **`FR-092` and `FR-093` get a step of their own** (§3.2), which
is more room than the single-page version gave them, not less. ⛔ **The mockup's own *"Do not revert
this"* is a different item** — the three-stand pass-schedule table — and it is untouched (§3.2).

### 3.1 What is common to all three stations

⛔ **One implementation each, no per-station copy.** Nothing in this list reads the station profile:

- The route, the shell child, station resolution and the refusal state
- The `[UIC §4.1]` layout frame and the nav rail
- **The wizard chrome** — tab strip, progressive unlock, prev/next, footer, progress, commit gate.
  The step *set* varies; the machine does not
- **The Pass Schedule step** — `[RCI §4.2]`: *"Identical pattern to Section 3.4"*, plus `FR-094`
- **The Pre-Run SPC step** — the same control and the same ⛔ *block-when-no-band* rule (`P-11`);
  only the measurands differ
- The gate/verdict model, the step-state machine, the error mapping and the commit path
- `CI-1` no tag before confirmation · `CI-2` records before tags · `CI-3` schedule copied not
  referenced · `CI-5` a non-recommended pick needs a reason · `CI-6` `INFLAT` at check-in only —
  `[RCI §5]` states these **without naming a line**, which is what makes them the shared contract

✅ **Three of these were already station-keyed before `D-60`**, which is why the merge is cheap:
`findCardSpecs(machineName, step)` looks its rows up **by station**, `CHECK_SPECS` is a
`[machineName][step]` map, and the fixtures are keyed by station.

### 3.2 What is station-specific

| | **FL1 / FL3 — rod** | **FL2 — spool** |
|---|---|---|
| Material and scan | rod alpha `R#####` | spool alpha, from the label **printed at FL1 output** (`FR-090`) |
| Pre-flight checks | **eight** (`[RCI §3.1]`) | **five** (`[RCI §4.1]`) |
| Visual inspection | **required** — 4 items, hard block to WIP Rejection, no bypass (`CI-4`) | ⛔ **absent.** `FR-095` is a *Must*; `TC-114` asserts the section is **absent**; `[RCI §4.3]` gives the reason — *"re-inspecting flattened wire on a bobbin would neither find the defects that inspection looks for nor be a meaningful check of anything"* |
| Payoff selector | applies; pre-filled and read-only when staged (`CHK005`, `CI-7`) | ⛔ **does not apply** — spools are not staged at a payoff |
| Machine inspections | Die Block DB1/DB2 · Rolling Mill FM1 · Lube & Safety ⚠ (`G125` — unrequired even here) | ⛔ **none defined.** FL2 has no drawing dies. ⛔ Do not invent them |
| SPC measurands | **diameter + ovality** (`CHK007`) | **gauge + width**, *"or confirmed from the FL1 production data"* |
| Incoming history | none — the run has not started (`[RCI §4.3]`) | **the FL1 gauge profile**: target line, tolerance band, weld markers, min/max/avg/std-dev/samples and an *all in spec* / *N out of spec* badge (`FR-093`, *Must*) |
| Source links | one rod | **source traceability** — each contributing rod with its footage range, and the induction weld rows between them with quality result and timestamp, read-only (`FR-092`, *Must*) |
| Pass schedule rows | FL1 four; **FL3 nine** — one unified schedule over FM1 **and** FM2 (`D6`) | ⛔ **exactly three stands** (`FR-094`) — the table below |
| Endpoint | `POST /checkin/rod` | `POST /checkin/spool` |
| Extra validation | — | **hybrid-origin** (`FR-091`, `OI-47`) — ⭐ **but the client answered on 9 Sep 2026 that FL3 produces COILS, NOT SPOOLS, so this validation has no case to catch.** Build the refusal path because `FR-091` and `OI-47` are still open, and ⛔ **do not build a UI that presumes an FL3-origin spool exists** |
| Tags pushed | FM1 (FL1); FM1 **and** FM2 in one batch (FL3) | FM2 S1/S2/S3 roll gaps and stand states, edger activation and edge type at S2/S3 (`FR-096`) |

⛔ **The FM2 stand set is a hard must-preserve, and it is the one thing DB5's mockup says "Do not
revert this" about** — it had briefly carried four rows and *"told builders to disregard this file"*:

| FM2 stand | Roller | Edger | Bypassable |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1** | 8″ | No | Yes |
| **S2** | 6″ | Yes | Yes |
| **S3** | 6″ | Yes | ⛔ **No — final gauge control** |

⛔ So `pass-schedule-table` must **never assume a row count** — FL1's four, FL3's nine and FL2's
three all come from the schedule the API returns, never from a per-station template. And `State`
must survive as one of `Active` / `Bypass` / `Skip` rather than collapsing to a boolean, which is
exactly what `FW-133 §3.2` insists on: **S1 and S2 are bypassable and S3 is not.**

⚠ **`lib-chart-canvas` and `lib-trace-panel` come back into scope — for FL2 only.** They were struck
from this story's AC 6 when it was rod-only, correctly: DB2 has zero canvas elements and `[RCI §4.3]`
says *"Gauge trace shown — none. The run has not started."* But **`FR-093` is a chart**, so FL2's
Spool Origin step needs one. ⛔ That is why `FW-081` (*gauge-trace-chart live streaming*, Phase 5,
in-progress) is now a dependency, and why the "no canvas on this screen" note in `§5.1a` is true of
FL1/FL3 and **false of FL2**.

⚠ **FL3 never does spool check-in.** `[RCI]`'s *"FL2 / FL3 spool check-in"* scope line is about
hybrid-**origin** spools arriving at FL2; `FS-11 §1` is explicit that on the FL3 route *"no
intermediate spool exists at all"*.

### 3.3 The station profile — the shape that makes it one component

**Follow the pattern the merged Active Run monitor already uses.** `LINE_PROFILES` in
`constants/line-profiles.constant.ts` with `LineProfile` in `interfaces/line-profile.interface.ts`
is how `D-55` turned three screens into one component with four per-line rows.

✅ **Measured in the built code, so this is a second instance of a working pattern rather than a new

## 7. Decisions this plan makes

### `P-07` The route is screen-first

⚠ **Superseded in part by `D-60`: the address is now `/flat-wire/checkin/:machineName`**, because
the screen is no longer rod-only. The reasoning below stands — screen-first over line-first — and
`D-60` additionally supersedes `[CMP §5.2]`'s `/flat-wire/line/FL2/checkin/spool`, which hard-codes
FL2 in the path.

**`/flat-wire/checkin/rod/:machineName`.** `[CMP §5.2]` lists DB2 as
`/flat-wire/line/:machineName/checkin/rod`, but its own `D-55` note calls the two coexisting
grammars **"a defect, not a style difference"** and writes the exit in terms: *"Either the siblings
become `/flat-wire/checkin/rod/:machineName` … or the split is recorded as deliberate."* The built
router already went screen-first (`flatline/:machineName`, `D-59`), and **DB2 is the first sibling
to be built** — so this is the cheapest possible moment to close the defect in the direction the
build has already taken.

**Fallback:** line-first, per `[CMP §5.2]`'s table. **Rejected** — it means reopening DB3's settled
address to add a `line/` prefix, and `D-59` closed that ten days ago. ⚠ The three remaining
`line/:machineName/...` routes in `[CMP §5.2]` must be restated to match; that is `[CMP]`'s edit,
not this plan's, and it is named in §9.

### `P-08` AC 2's supervisor override is descoped

A failed inspection or an out-of-spec SPC is a **hard block routed to WIP Rejection with no
bypass** — `[RCI §3.1]` check 6, rule `CI-4`, and `[API §4.6]`'s `422 INSPECTION_FAILED` all say
so. The credential path is not built.

⚠ **Step 2's deviation survives.** A non-recommended schedule completes the step, needs **no
credential**, and requires a **free-text reason** plus the Ops-review flag (`CI-5`). So this removes
the credential path, **not the deviation concept**.

**Fallback:** build it. **Rejected, and note the cost is not the reason.** ✅ The dialog is
effectively **free** — `SupervisorLoginComponent` already exists in `projects/shared`, is exported
from `public-api.ts`, authenticates against `SharedApiService.validateSupervisor`, and is opened by
`SharedCommonPopupModel.supervisorOverridePopup()`. It is rejected because **`POST /checkin/rod`
has no `supervisorOverride` field** — the staging endpoints have one, check-in does not — so an
authorised deviation would be collected, discarded, and the run recorded as **clean**. Free UI over
a missing column is worse than no UI.

⚠ **Two consequences to state plainly.** Unscheduled material cannot be run from DB2 in MVP-1, so
`Q25`'s override half goes with this; and if the client wants the override, `[API §4.6]` and
`FW-157` need the field first, at which point the UI is a small add.

### `P-09` Steps 4–6 are client-side gates only

They block the button and persist nothing, pending **`G125`**. ⛔ Say this in the handoff and in
`[UIC]`: *the screen renders* is not *the screen works*.

**Fallback:** drop them from the wizard. **Rejected** — `[TB §7]`'s AC and `FS-07 §4` both name six
steps, and removing an accepted AC on our own reading is the client's call, not this plan's.

### `P-10` Acknowledge lands on DB3, for now

`FR-079a` and `[RCI §3.7]` target **DB2A**, both `[PROPOSED]`, with `OI-109` open. But `FW-N01`
(DB2A) is not-started and `[TRP §7]` puts DB2A **out of trial scope**, so DB3 is the interim — which
is also `OI-109`'s own recommendation (*land on Dashboard 3 and offer a prominent return to the
staging station*). `OI-109` records that being wrong here **costs a preference rather than a
defect**: no record, status, tag push or broadcast changes with it.

### `P-11` Tolerances are injected, and absent means blocked

The band covers all four attributes per rule `CI-9` and arrives with the rod, never as a constant.
⛔ **If it is absent, step 3 blocks** with a *tolerances unavailable* message rather than defaulting
to anything. `Q22` + **`G121`**: the values arrived on 8 Sep 2026 and are per **vendor × size
band**, which `AlloyProperty`'s alloy grain cannot hold — so `CHK007` still cannot fire, and
seeding against the current shape would be **wrong data rather than absent data**.

**Fallback:** the mockup's `0.370` / `0.380` / `0.003`. **Rejected** — `[VAL §7.5a]` forbids
inferring validation from the mockup's markup, and a hard-coded band would look correct while
accepting material a vendor rejects.

### `P-12` The nav rail

`[UIC §3.6]` turns a mockup's bottom action bar into the left rail. DB2's footer is the wizard's
commit and stays a footer; the screen's **task** actions are the mockup's More-Options tiles, so
those become the rail. ⛔ Entries whose destinations do not exist are `isActive: false`, **not
omitted**, so the operator sees the shape of the screen's actions from day one — all of them are
inert today. `screenKey = 'rod-checkin'`, held in a constant and ⛔ **deliberately not the route
segment**, because it is stamped into every DOM id. No `(expandedChange)` handler — there is no
canvas to resize.

changed is only where they are served from."* `P-54` answered *where* with **"the receiving team's
own surface"**, and ⛔ **that surface exists in none of the four checkouts** — not as an endpoint, a
service, a screen or a team contact. A requirement with no host is not served; it is deferred
silently.

**So the read is hosted here**, as a `FlatWireDB` **synonym** over the shared schema plus a
read-only repository method, specified as `[API §4.22]` and built by
[`FW-N35`](../../FS-15-shared-schema-boundary/BE/FW-N35.md). ⛔ **It never calls
`sp_IngestRodFromCoils`** — that procedure writes, requires `@@TRANCOUNT > 0`, and `FR-530` forbids
a lookup creating records.

⚠ **This qualifies `P-53` and `P-54` rather than reversing them, and the distinction is the whole
decision.** Rod **receiving** stays another team's — no `/rod/**` surface, no
`RodReceivingController`, no ingestion here. What flat wire hosts is the **check-in gate**: a
read-only existence check on the coil master, addressed under `checkin/`, which is this service's
own subject. ⛔ A superseding note is appended where `P-54` lives (`FW-138` §7); its text is not
edited.

⚠ **`P-54`'s option-2 reasoning was factually wrong**, and it matters because that reasoning is
still quoted in three places (`[API §3.2]`, `FW-138` twice). It held that `CoilCheckin`'s
`getCheckinCoilInfo` and `getCoilStatus` *"serve the shared-schema fields — alpha, alloy, temper,
diameter, weights, status, location, received-at."* **Measured 10 Sep 2026:** `getCheckinCoilInfo`
returns `MfgOrderNo`, `SeqNo`, `OpLetter` and three booleans; `getCoilStatus` returns a single
`VARCHAR(6)` status. Between them they serve **status alone**. Recorded as `G132`.

**Fallback:** wait for the receiving team to specify a surface. **Rejected** — nothing names that
team or that contract, `FW-233`'s 6 h were released, and the screen cannot validate a scan
meanwhile. ⛔ The cost of this decision is stated rather than hidden: flat wire now reads a table
whose lifecycle it does not own, exactly as `D-32` already permits for the `coils` R-series row.

### `P-352` Four bundle cells populate; two render *not available*

**Incoming Bundle Information has six cells and `coils` can serve four** — Alloy (resolved through
`Alloys`), Temper, Gross weight, Net weight. ⛔ **Heat / Cast no. has no column anywhere**
(`OI-117`, and `coil_origin_code` is a one-character origin flag, not a heat number) and ⛔ **the
supplier name is unmapped** (`coils.vendor_no` is a two-character code) — `G130`.

**The grid ships with four populated cells and two rendering an explicit *not available*.**

**Fallback:** hold the whole grid until every cell has a source. **Rejected** — it withholds four
correct values to avoid admitting two missing ones. ⛔ **The rejected alternative that matters more
is inventing them:** deriving a heat number from `coil_origin_code`, or showing `vendor_no`'s code
as a supplier name, would put a plausible wrong value on a traceability screen. A blank cell is
recoverable; a fabricated one is not.

### `P-353` Shared reads reach `FlatWireDB` as synonyms, not views

**The convention is a SYNONYM, and `[DBD §6.6]` said the opposite until 10 Sep 2026.** Measured in
`ual-database`: `Alloys` is a synonym in **six** databases and a view in **exactly one**
(`PlanningDB`); `coils` is a synonym in six, every one targeting the base table `[CommonDB]..[coils]`.
§6.6 is corrected, and the `FlatWireDB..Alloys` **view** it specified is retired.

**The rule, stated once:** **a synonym when the read is a pass-through; a view only when columns must
be reshaped or renamed.** ⛔ **A synonym targets an actual table, never another synonym** — SQL
Server does not permit the chain, and it fails at *query* time rather than at creation, which is why
`FlatWireDB`'s `coils` synonym points at `CommonDB` and ⛔ **never at `proddb..coils`**, which is
itself a synonym.

⚠ **`35_FlatWireDB_View_WIPStations.sql` stays a view** under that rule — it renames `WIPStation` →
`Station` and trims the padding, which a synonym cannot do. ⛔ Do not convert it for consistency.
⚠ **A synonym absorbs nothing**, so the padding trim, the `smallint → DECIMAL(8,2)` weight cast and
the alloy resolution live in the one Dapper query that reads them. ⚠ That resolution joins on the alloy **NAME** — `alloy_idx` matches nothing against real data (`G133`).

**Fallback:** build the `FlatWireDB..Coils` view §6.6's convention implied. **Rejected on
measurement** — the premise was wrong, and a view binds at creation time, so it also fails outright
on any instance without the shared databases, where a synonym deploys and defers.
