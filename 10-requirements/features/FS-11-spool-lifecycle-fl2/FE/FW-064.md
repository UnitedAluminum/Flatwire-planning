## Added by `D-55`, 7 September 2026 — what the spool check-in screen must show

⚠ **Adjacent scope.** `D-55` re-scopes the Active Run monitor, not this screen. But the FL1→FL2
traceability chain becomes visible to the operator **here**, at the moment a spool number is entered,
and two things it must show are not in `GET /spools`'s response yet — see **`FW-179` §7**.

- [ ] On entering a spool number, the screen resolves and displays **the spool's child alphas** —
      one per source rod segment (`SpoolTraceability.ChildAlpha`) — not only the source rod alphas.
- [ ] It displays **every order the spool is committed to**, not one. A spool off FL1 may carry two or
      more while FL2 runs one at a time, so the operator chooses; the selection lands on
      `SpoolCheckin.OrderId`.

⛔ **Blocked upstream in two places:** the response shape (`FW-179` §7) and `G54` — until `FW-230`
and `FW-231` ship together there are no child alphas to display. ⚠ **The multi-order half is a
contested client position** (`FW-207`'s banner); if it resolves to one order per spool, the second
criterion above disappears.
