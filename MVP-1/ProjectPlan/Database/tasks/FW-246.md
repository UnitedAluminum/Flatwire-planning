## 2. The four details

### 2.1 `G41` — line-aware, and not the generalisation

The constraint reads *"no `FM1` row may be `Bypass`"* on **every** schedule. An FL2-standalone
run is fed an already-flattened spool; FL1's 12″ mill is not in its material path — so every
FL2 schedule is forced to carry an `Active` `FM1` row that describes a machine the material
never reaches.

⛔ **`[GAP]` explicitly forbids generalising into *"a component's `Stand.LineId` must equal its
schedule's `LineId`"*.** That rule is wrong for **FL3**, which is FL1 feeding FL2 — a hybrid
schedule legitimately carries components from both lines. The forbidden rule would make FL3
unauthorable while fixing FL2.

**The narrow fix:** make the existing constraint conditional on the schedule's line —
*"`FM1` may not be `Bypass` **on a line whose material path includes it**"* — which is FL1 and
FL3, not FL2. ⚠ `PassScheduleComponent` must be able to see its schedule's `LineId`; if it
cannot, this becomes a trigger or a denormalised column, and **that choice is the story's real
content**. Prefer the column: a `CHECK` is declarative and a trigger is not.

### 2.2 `G55` — one value, and the safe direction is `3`

Two artifacts disagree about a number on a column with **no FK**, so `TC-020`'s membership diff
passes — it compares *sets*, and there is no set to compare against.

| Source | Says | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `CK_SpoolCheckin_PayoffPos` (`04_Runs.sql:690`) | **`1`** | ⚠ Comment at `:679`: *"copied from `RodCheckin`"* |
| `PayoffPosition` seed (`01_Lookup.sql:285`) | **`3`** | Labelled *"Traversing take-up (**FL2**)"* |
| `CanonicalEnums.cs` | **`3`** | `TraversingTakeup` |

**Two against one, and the one has a stated provenance that explains the error** — it was
copied from the rod check-in constraint, where payoffs really are 1 and 2. FL2 does not have a
VPS payoff at all; it has a traversing take-up.

⚠ **The blocker is real but narrow.** *"FL2 has ONE payoff"* is not in doubt. **Which one** is,
and the evidence points one way. **Record the internal decision** (the card permits it —
*"or an internal decision recorded as one"*) rather than waiting: `P-163`.

⛔ **The `CHECK` is not the only thing to change.** Any seeded `SpoolCheckin` row carrying `1`
must move to `3`, or the new constraint rejects the seed.

### 2.3 `G52` may need no DDL

`PinRole='Sole'` passes its `CHECK` and matches none of the sequence validator's four tiers, so
`minTier` is undefined over it while `FR-546` requires an out-of-tier rod to be **refused**.
⚠ `PinnedBoth` is fine — it is explicitly a member of both sets.

AC 3 offers two routes and they land in different repositories:

- **`Sole` joins a tier** → the fix is in `RodOrderAllocation.md` §3's partition and in
  `FW-226`'s validator. **No DDL.**
- **`Sole` leaves the `CHECK`** → DDL here, plus every row carrying it.

**Decide before writing DDL.** ⚠ `Sole` almost certainly *means* something — a rod that is the
only one for its order — so removing it from the vocabulary is the more destructive answer.
**This story's likely deliverable for `G52` is a decision and a demonstration, not a
constraint.**

### 2.4 Three stories, six files, one sprint

`FW-244`, `FW-245` and this story all edit `03_Materials`, `04_Runs`, `05_QualityOutput`, `06`
and `07` in `S1`. **Sequence them.** ⚠ This story and `FW-244` both touch `WipRejection`'s
constraints — `FW-244`'s B9 adds a `CHECK` on `RejectionReason` while this one closes RI holes
on the same table.

## 4. Decisions this plan makes

> The `P-##` series belongs to [`Backend/tasks/`](../../Backend/tasks/)
> and is continuous across the repository; `P-01`–`P-162` precede this story.

### `P-163` — FL2's payoff is `3 · TraversingTakeup`, recorded as an internal decision

§2.2. Two artifacts say `3`; the one saying `1` carries a comment recording that it was
**copied from `RodCheckin`**, where 1 and 2 are correct. FL2 has a traversing take-up, not a
VPS payoff.

The card permits *"an internal decision recorded as one"*, and waiting blocks three other
repairs in the same story.

**Fallback:** if the client answers `1`, the change is the seed and `CanonicalEnums.cs` instead
— **two artifacts move rather than one**, which is the second reason to prefer `3`.

### `P-164` — `G41` is fixed narrowly, by line, and the forbidden generalisation is not written

§2.1. `[GAP]` rules it out because it breaks **FL3**, which legitimately spans both lines.
Recorded because the generalisation is the obvious fix and looks more principled than the
narrow one.

### `P-165` — `G52`'s likely deliverable is a decision, not DDL

§2.3. `Sole` means something; removing it from the vocabulary is more destructive than giving
it a tier. **Settle it before step 5**, since it decides whether this gap costs DDL at all.
