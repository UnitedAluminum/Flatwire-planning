## 2. The four details

### 2.1 ⛔ The second shared-schema cancellation, and why it is missed

`D-32` is read as being about `FW-001` — the column renames and the `INFLAT` status. **It also
cancels this card's `coils` carry-forward columns**, and the card says so explicitly because the
omission is easy.

⚠ **Two independent facts make it safe:**
- The replacement design is **`FlatWireDB`-local** and was **delivered on 26 Jul 2026**, before
  `D-32` — so cancelling costs nothing.
- The snake_case names in the May 2026 design document (`footage_run_to_date`,
  `remaining_weight_estimate`, `source_rod_alpha`) are **proposals, not the schema** — and after
  `D-32` they are proposals **against a table this module no longer alters**.

⛔ **So a developer finding those names in the design document must not go looking for them in
`coils`.** The delivered columns are `Rod.FootageRunToDate`, `Rod.RemainingWeightEstimateLb` and
`SpoolProcessing.SourceRodAlpha`.

### 2.2 The 16 h falls to zero, and the card guards against the wrong inference

The rate card was re-derived **28 h → 12 h**, and the note is unusually careful:

> *"the 16 h … **falls to zero rather than shrinking** — the local columns that replace them were
> delivered in the DDL on 26 Jul 2026, so there is **no residual schema work hiding inside the
> removed line**."*

⚠ **And then the important half:** *"if wiring the carry-forward write paths needs time of its
own it must be **re-priced as a new line**, not assumed to have been inside this one."*

⛔ **That wiring is `FW-174`'s**, and this record cannot tell whether it is costed there. **Do not
absorb it here by assumption** — that is exactly the arithmetic `[CE §8]` calls *"making the
arithmetic lie"*.

### 2.3 Two polymorphic columns, no FK, and that is documented rather than fixed

`WipRejection.MaterialAlpha` holds **a rod or a spool alpha**; `RodCheckout.PartialSpoolAlpha`
holds a spool alpha. **Neither has an FK.**

⚠ **`MaterialAlpha`'s polymorphism is the reason** — a single FK cannot point at two parents, and
splitting it into two nullable columns would allow both to be set.

⛔ **So the integrity is the aggregate's**, and `G17` already records that rod-alpha references
are logical across the boundary. ⚠ **`G49` B5 compounds it**: `WipRejection` also has **no
`SpcCheckpointId` / `RunPauseEventId` / trigger-source column**, so *"the rejection and the
checkpoint cannot disagree"* is **unenforceable** today.

### 2.4 `G24`'s state has moved and `G21`'s has closed

The card lists **`G24`** and **`G21` / `OQ-23`** as blockers. Both need reading in their current
state:

## 4. Decisions this plan makes

> The `P-##` series belongs to [`Backend/tasks/`](../../Backend/tasks/)
> and is continuous across the repository; `P-01`–`P-238` precede this story.

### `P-239` — the cancelled 16 h is not silently reassigned to the write paths

§2.2, and the card asks for exactly this. **Absorbing cancelled hours into adjacent work is how a
re-derivation stops being auditable** — `[CE §8]`'s *"makes the arithmetic lie"*.

**If `FW-174`'s carry-forward wiring is uncosted, it is a new line for `FW-249` to pick up.**

### `P-240` — `MaterialAlpha` stays polymorphic and unconstrained

§2.3. Two nullable FK columns would permit both being set and would still need an aggregate rule;
one polymorphic column with a documented contract is honest about where the integrity lives.

⚠ **`G49` B5 must still land** — without a trigger source, the rejection cannot be reconciled with
its checkpoint at all.
